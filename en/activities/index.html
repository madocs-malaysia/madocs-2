CCC7, PCC2-1 & TTT Touchstone, Batu Pahat, Johor

CCC

CCC6, Hotel Maluri, Cheras, Kuala Lumpur

1 June 2025

Comprehensive Circumcision Course 6 (CCC6) on Sunday 1 June 2025 at Hotel Maluri, Cheras. Lectures on anatomy and local anaesthesia, the Guillotine, Teknoklem and stapler methods, live procedure demonstrations, and hands-on training at three stations on real patients under facilitator supervision. The day before, a Teknoklem Train The Trainer (TTT) was held at the same hotel.

About MADOCS

The Malaysian Doctors of Circumcision Society (MADOCS) is a professional association that raises the standards of safe circumcision practice through education, training, advocacy and professional collaboration.
