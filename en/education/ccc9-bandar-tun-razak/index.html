Comprehensive Circumcision Course (CCC 9)

CCC 9 takes place on Sunday, 13 December 2026 at Masjid Saidina Uthman Ibn Affan, Bandar Tun Razak. Four methods: forceps-guided, Smart Clamp, Tekno Klem and ZSR Stapler. Each participant chooses one method and performs two practical cases.

CCC 9Registration open

Photograph from a previous MADOCS programme (CCC6, Hotel Maluri Cheras, 2025) — not a CCC9 photograph.

CCC 9 will be held on Sunday, 13 December 2026, from 8.00 am to 5.00 pm, at Masjid Saidina Uthman Ibn Affan, Jalan Budiman, Bandar Tun Razak, Cheras.

Four methods are offered: forceps-guided, Smart Clamp (clamp method), Tekno Klem (glue method) and ZSR Stapler (stapler method). Each participant chooses one method and performs two practical cases in that method.

The fee covers lectures, meals and two practical cases. Participant rates: Forceps-guided RM900 (MADOCS members RM850) · Smart Clamp RM970 (RM920) · Tekno Klem RM1,030 (RM980) · ZSR Stapler RM1,500 (RM1,450).

CPD points will be awarded. Each method is limited to 10 participants — register early to secure a place.

Payment to Maybank account 5629 9102 8408 (Malaysian Doctors of Circumcision Society).

CCC 9 is organised by MADOCS with the support of Nano Medic Care Sdn Bhd.
