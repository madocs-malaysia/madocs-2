MADOCS Organisation

Governance of the organisation: organisational chart, Advisors and Executive Committee.

Organisational Structure

MADOCS is governed by 13 board members led by a President, Deputy President, Secretary, Assistant Secretary, Treasurer and 8 Committee Members. The organisation is also assisted by 2 Advisors.

2Advisors
1President
1Deputy President
1Secretary
1Assistant Secretary
1Treasurer
8Committee Members

The committee is responsible for organising and supervising the society’s day-to-day activities and for deciding matters affecting its running, within the general policy laid down by the Annual General Meeting.

About MADOCS

The Malaysian Doctors of Circumcision Society (MADOCS) is a professional association that raises the standards of safe circumcision practice through education, training, advocacy and professional collaboration.
