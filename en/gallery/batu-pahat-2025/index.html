Programme album at The Katerina Hotel, Batu Pahat: the Privileging Course for Circumcision Part 2 (PCC2-1) and Comprehensive Circumcision Course 7 (CCC7) on 13 September 2025, and the Touchstone Train The Trainer on 14 September 2025.

About MADOCS

The Malaysian Doctor of Circumcision Society (MADOCS) is a professional association that raises the standards of safe circumcision practice through education, training, advocacy and professional collaboration.
