MADOCS Programme 2025

Wednesday, 31 December 2025

Album of MADOCS programmes through 2025: comprehensive circumcision courses, the privileging course, guideline meetings with the Ministry of Health, sunathon and the national symposium. Patient and procedure photographs are not published.

Photos were taken during MADOCS programmes and are published for the organisation’s documentation. Parents, guardians or participants who wish a particular photo to be removed may contact the MADOCS secretariat. Back to all albums.

About MADOCS

The Malaysian Doctors of Circumcision Society (MADOCS) is a professional association that raises the standards of safe circumcision practice through education, training, advocacy and professional collaboration.
