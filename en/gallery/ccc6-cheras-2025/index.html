CCC6 — Hotel Maluri, Cheras, Kuala Lumpur

1 Jun 2025 · Hotel Maluri, Cheras, Kuala Lumpur

Comprehensive Circumcision Course 6 (CCC6) on 1 June 2025: lectures on anatomy and anaesthesia, live demonstrations of the Guillotine, Teknoklem and ZSR Stapler methods, and hands-on training at three stations.

Photos were taken during MADOCS programmes and are published for the organisation’s documentation. Parents, guardians or participants who wish a particular photo to be removed may contact the MADOCS secretariat. Back to all albums.

About MADOCS

The Malaysian Doctor of Circumcision Society (MADOCS) is a professional association that raises the standards of safe circumcision practice through education, training, advocacy and professional collaboration.
