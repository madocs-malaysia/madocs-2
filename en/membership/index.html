Why join MADOCS

MADOCS brings together doctors who are serious about raising the quality of circumcision practice. Membership provides access to structured clinical training, a professional network and educational resources.

Key membership benefits:

  • A professional community of doctors involved in the practice of circumcision
  • Clinical education and structured training
  • Practical training and workshop opportunities
  • Professional network and knowledge sharing
  • Member resources and reference materials
  • Continuous professional development (CPD)

Application process

Step 1Complete the application form online and submit proof of payment to the secretariat.
Step 2The secretariat verifies your details and documents: MMC registration, APC and practice details.
Step 3The application is tabled at the Committee meeting for approval.
Step 4You will be notified, and your membership will be active starting from the approval date.
If your application is rejected, you will be notified with an explanation, and your payment will be refunded in full.
Step 5You will be issued a membership number and the membership certificate by the secretariat.
Step 6Except for Life Members and Honorary Members, the secretariat will remind you to renew your membership annually.

Frequently asked questions

Who is eligible to join MADOCS?

Malaysian citizens: medical doctors registered with the MMC (Ordinary Member), medical personnel involved in circumcision procedures (Associate Member) and medical students (Student Member).

How much are the membership fees?

For the 2026 promotion: a registration fee of RM100 and life membership at RM900 (normally RM1,000) for new members or upgrades. Further details are in the Life Membership Promotion section on this page.

How does the application process work?

Submit the application form; the Membership Committee will review it and contact the applicant.

What is an Honorary Member?

Honorary Members are surgeons, doctors or individuals who have contributed to the field of circumcision and are recognised by the society. They are appointed specially by the Committee and pay no fees.

Who is eligible to vote at the General Meeting?

Ordinary Members only. Associate and Student Members are not eligible to vote

Can I do the C&P without a MADOCS membership?

Yes, you can do the C&P without a MADOCS membership. The registration for C&P is separate from MADOCS membership. MADOCS works with the Ministry of Health to provide C&P courses, and MADOCS members will receive earlier updates for C&P courses.
